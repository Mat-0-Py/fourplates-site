Before you start

Four Plates records your training. It doesn't give medical advice, and you choose every weight.

Lifting carries a risk of injury. Warm up, keep good form, use safety bars or a spotter for heavy lifts, and stop if something hurts.

Before starting a new training programme, get advice from a doctor or a qualified fitness professional, especially if you have a health condition, are pregnant or are new to exercise.

In the app, everyone reads this and taps “I understand” before they start.

Four Plates Pro subscriptions and one-off purchase

Logging is free forever. Four Plates Pro adds the longer view and the extra features shown on the Free and Pro table.

The UK prices are:

  • Monthly: £2.99 per month.
  • Annual: £19.99 per year, with one free week.
  • Pay once: £29.99 as a one-off purchase until 31 January 2027 (the launch price), then £49.99 from 1 February 2027.

Payment is taken by Apple through your Apple Account. The annual plan's free week is followed by the annual charge unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the free week ends.

Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. The one-off purchase does not renew.

The one-off purchase unlocks Four Plates Pro for as long as you use Four Plates. Nothing in Four Plates runs on our servers, so if Four Plates is ever withdrawn from the App Store, the copy you have keeps working as it is, Four Plates Pro included, and your training stays on your devices and in your iCloud. We can't promise that future versions of iOS will keep running an app that's no longer updated.

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel in your Apple Account's subscription settings. Deleting Four Plates does not cancel a subscription.

Your sessions and history remain yours whether or not you have Four Plates Pro. You can still log, view your history, import, export and sync your data.
