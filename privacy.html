Four Plates for iPhone

Privacy policy

Four Plates doesn't collect your data.

Your training

Your training is stored on your device. If you use iCloud, it's also stored in your private iCloud database so it syncs between your devices. There it's end-to-end encrypted, with keys kept in your iCloud Keychain, so only your own devices can read it: we can't, and neither can Apple.

Your sessions, sets, routines and custom exercises belong to you. Importing history, exporting it and syncing it through iCloud are free.

No tracking

There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account.

You do not need to create a Four Plates account to log your training or use iCloud sync.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

You manage a Four Plates Pro subscription in your Apple Account's subscription settings. The subscription terms explain the available plans and renewal.

Widgets and Apple Watch

The widget reads a small summary the app writes on your device. If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends it the session in progress directly; the Watch keeps no training of its own. Nothing leaves your phone except through your own iCloud and to your own Apple Watch.

That summary lets the widget show your training without opening the app's training database.

Your choices

You can export your history as a plain CSV file from Settings in Four Plates. Your history remains available whether or not you have Four Plates Pro.

For help with your data or iCloud sync, visit Support.
